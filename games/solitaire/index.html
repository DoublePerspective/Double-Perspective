A moment to learn

Klondike: Build descending columns in alternating colours. Only kings fill empty columns. Build the four suit foundations from ace to king. Tap the stock to draw or recycle.

FreeCell: All cards start face up. Build descending columns in alternating colours; any card can fill an empty column. Four free cells each hold one card. Moving a sequence requires enough empty cells and columns to move it legally.

Pyramid: Remove exposed kings on their own, or two exposed cards totalling 13. Ace = 1, jack = 11, queen = 12. A card is exposed when both cards below it are gone. You can pair the top waste card with an exposed pyramid card. Clear the pyramid to win.

Spider: Build descending sequences. Move ordered sequences of the same suit; any card or sequence can fill an empty column. Complete king-to-ace runs disappear. Fill empty columns before dealing one new card to every column. Complete eight runs to win.

TriPeaks: Tap an exposed card one rank higher or lower than the waste card. Ace connects to king or two. Draw when stuck; the stock cannot be recycled. Clear all three peaks to win.

Tap a card to select it, then a destination or matching card. Double tap a top card to send it to its foundation. Games save automatically. Some deals cannot be won.
