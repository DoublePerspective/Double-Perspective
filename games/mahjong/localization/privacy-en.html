Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Android games made by Double Perspective (“we”): Zenith & Zero Sudoku and Zenith & Zero Sudoku Pro, Umbral Purge (free and premium editions), and this website.

Zenith & Zero collection

This policy also covers Zenith & Zero Solitaire, Word Search, Crossword and Mahjong, each in Free and Paid editions. All five games store gameplay progress and preferences on your device. Android backup may include eligible app data under your backup settings. Paid collection editions contain no advertising SDK and request no internet permission. Free collection editions support banner ads only, with Google’s consent controls. The test packages currently use Google test ad identifiers; production advertising will be configured separately.

Where Google requires privacy options, use the Privacy choices button in the Free game’s main menu. Opening external support or privacy links uses your browser and its own network access.

Who we are

Double Perspective is a business registered in Québec, Canada (Québec enterprise number, NEQ: 2282699877). In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Double Perspective.

The short version

  • We don’t ask for your name, email or any account, and we don’t run any game servers.
  • Your progress, scores and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • Free editions show ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Paid editions (Zenith & Zero Sudoku Pro, Umbral Purge premium) show no ads. Zenith & Zero Sudoku Pro has no internet access at all.

Information stored on your device

Zenith & Zero Sudoku saves your puzzle in progress, your top 10 scores (including the name you type for a score), statistics and settings. Umbral Purge saves your progress, unlocks, settings and the run in progress. This stays on your phone. If you use Android’s backup, your phone may include it in your Google backup; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, and we can’t see it.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. In Zenith & Zero Sudoku you can also use Settings › Clear top 10 and stats.

Ads in the free editions (Google AdMob)

The free editions use the Google Mobile Ads SDK. According to Google, it collects and shares with Google:

  • IP address, which can give an approximate location: to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Advertising ID, app set ID and similar device identifiers: to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches and taps on ads: to measure ads and for analytics.
  • Diagnostic information about the app and the ads SDK: to keep ads working and for analytics.

This data is sent encrypted. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and places with similar rules, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google’s consent tool. You can change your choice any time; in Zenith & Zero Sudoku it is under Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Google’s use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Paid editions are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we don’t receive your payment details.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state (free editions): to load ads.
  • Vibration (Zenith & Zero Sudoku): a short buzz when you make a mistake, which you can turn off in Settings.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Website

This website has no contact form, user accounts, or tracking scripts. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may process technical connection information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver the pages.
