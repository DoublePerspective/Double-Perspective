THE NIGHT FIGHTS BACK.

Umbral Purge is a dark fantasy survivor roguelite built around constant pressure and meaningful choices. Your weapons fight automatically while you dodge danger, collect experience, and shape your build through upgrades and evolutions.

THE JOURNEY / FIVE WORLDS

EVERY WORLD CHANGES THE FIGHT.

Travel through five distinct regions. Each brings its own enemies and environmental dangers.

01 ✿

Meadow

Waves 1–10

Open ground and the first relentless hordes. Learn to move, collect experience, and build your arsenal.

02 ☀

Scorched Desert

Waves 11–20

Cacti and sand strikes turn the battlefield into a test of positioning.

03 ❄

Frozen Lake

Waves 21–30

Slippery movement and falling icicles make every step matter.

04 ♨

Ashlands

Waves 31–40

Lava and eruptions threaten even the strongest builds.

05 ✧

Umbral Depths

Waves 41+

Deeper darkness and overlapping dangers push survival to the limit.
