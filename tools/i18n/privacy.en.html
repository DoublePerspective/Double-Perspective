LEGAL & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy

Double Perspective does not collect personal information from players. Our games and apps have no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no server of our own. Your saves stay on your device. The only information that leaves your device is what Google needs to show an ad in games that have ads, kept to the minimum Google allows, and what app stores handle for downloads and purchases.

What this policy covers

  • Zenith & Zero: Solitaire, Sudoku, Word Search, Crossword, Mahjong, Nonograms and Cribbage, on Android. Free with ads; the optional Pro upgrade removes them.
  • Umbral Purge: PC on Steam. No ads.
  • Estivite and Sac-à-Do: Android and browser. No internet access, no ads. Details: Estivite, Sac-à-Do.
  • This website.

What we collect

Nothing, automatically. We don't receive your saves, scores, settings, projects, trips or play history. A name you type for a local score stays on your device.

If you email us, we receive your address, your message and any attachments. We use them only to reply, never for marketing, and delete them once the request is handled.

Saves and backups

App saves, records (such as best times and daily streaks) and settings are stored on your device. Estivite and Sac-à-Do website tests use browser storage. If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may back up Zenith & Zero saves to your own Google account; we can't see those backups. Estivite and Sac-à-Do turn Android backup off. Uninstalling an app or clearing its storage deletes its data.

Ads (Zenith & Zero on Android)

Ads come from Google AdMob, set to the least data Google allows:

  • non-personalized ads only: ads are not chosen from your interests or history;
  • the games don't use your advertising ID, and Android's ad-interest (Topics) and ad-measurement features are turned off.

To deliver and count each ad, Google still receives your IP address (which gives a general location), an app set ID that only links our own apps on your device, your device type and Android version, whether you saw or tapped the ad, and basic diagnostics. Google uses this to show the ad, limit repeats and prevent fraud (Google's AdMob data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy). We only see totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned.

Where the law requires it (for example in Europe), Google asks for your choice first, and a Privacy choices button lets you change it. With Pro, or in apps without ads, no ad is ever requested. Test versions released up to November 7, 2026 (Zenith & Zero 2.1 and Cribbage 1.1.1, plus an early Umbral Purge Android test that is no longer updated) still use Google's standard ad settings, which include the advertising ID.

Purchases and stores

Google Play and Steam handle downloads, payments and refunds under their own policies; we never see your card details. For Pro, the game only receives Google's confirmation that you own it, kept on your device so it works offline. Stores give us sales and crash totals, not personal profiles. See the Valve Privacy Policy for Steam.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives connection information such as IP addresses (GitHub Privacy Statement). There are no trackers, analytics or ad scripts. The Estivite and Sac-à-Do browser tests, and the language menu, save their settings only in your browser.

Children

Our games are for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly receive information from children; if you think a child emailed us, tell us and we'll delete it.
