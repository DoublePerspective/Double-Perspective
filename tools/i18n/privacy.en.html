LEGAL & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy

No Lirecho account is required, and Double Perspective does not receive your library or reading history. We do not operate a library-upload server or add our own in-app analytics or crash-reporting service. App data is stored on your device by default. Optional features can share data with a destination you choose or with third-party services, as explained below. Advertising providers, app stores and our website host also process information under their own policies.

What this policy covers

  • Zenith & Zero: Solitaire, Sudoku, Word Search, Crossword, Mahjong, Nonograms and Cribbage, on Android. Free with ads; the optional Pro upgrade removes them.
  • Lirecho: audiobook and ebook reader on Android. Free with ads; the optional Pro upgrade removes them, and a rewarded video can enable 24 ad-free hours.
  • Umbral Purge: PC on Steam. No ads.
  • Estivite and Sac-à-Do: Android. No internet access, no ads. Details: Estivite, Sac-à-Do.
  • This website.

What we collect

Nothing, automatically. We don't receive your saves, scores, settings, projects, trips, library files, listening history or reading history. A name you type for a local score stays on your device.

If you email us, we receive your address, your message and any attachments. We use them only to reply, never for marketing, and delete them once the request is handled.

Saves and backups

App saves, records and settings are stored on your device. Lirecho stores library entries, reading and listening positions, bookmarks, highlights, notes, collections, series information and listening statistics locally. Recommendations based on your unfinished books are calculated on-device. Book and audio files are opened from locations you choose and are never uploaded to Double Perspective.

If you export a Lirecho backup, the destination you choose receives a JSON file containing book titles, authors and file identifiers, progress, bookmarks, quotations, notes, translations, collections, metadata and settings. Optional shared-folder synchronization writes a backup snapshot for each device and reads the other devices’ snapshots. The selected storage provider or folder-sync app may copy these files to its servers or other devices under its own policy. Lirecho does not encrypt the JSON snapshots; protect access to the folder. The snapshots do not contain the ebook or audio files, which must be added separately on each device.

Disconnecting synchronization stops future scheduled syncs but leaves existing snapshots in the shared folder. You can remove them through your storage provider. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data, but does not remove original books, exported backups or shared-folder copies. If Android backup is enabled, Android may back up Zenith & Zero and Lirecho app data to your Google account; we cannot access those backups. Estivite and Sac-à-Do disable Android backup.

Reading and study tools in Lirecho

Lirecho processes scanned page images and selected text on your device for text recognition (OCR), language detection and translation using Google ML Kit. Google states that these APIs do not send the input or results to its servers. ML Kit may connect to Google to download or update models and sends performance and usage metrics to Google. See Google ML Kit terms and privacy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Read Aloud uses the speech engine and voice installed or selected on your Android device. Offline voices process speech on your device. If you choose a voice that requires the internet, its provider may process the text under its own privacy policy.

Opening a Free Books link takes you to an external archive or download service, which handles your visit and download under its own privacy policy. Lirecho opens files you choose to import. If you choose to look up text in another app or export your notes, the selected app or destination receives the information you choose to share.

Optional online discovery and casting in Lirecho

When you choose to search book archives or find a cover, Lirecho sends the search text, such as a title or author, to Open Library, with Internet Archive as a fallback. Requests for search results, preview covers and downloaded covers reveal your IP address and normal connection information to those services. Your ebook/audio files and notes are not sent for these searches. See the Internet Archive privacy policy, which also covers Open Library. External Free Books links open sites whose own privacy rules apply to your visit and download.

When you start Chromecast playback, Lirecho shares the selected audiobook audio and playback metadata, such as its title and chapter, with the selected Cast receiver. Local files are streamed from your phone over HTTP on the local network; that audio stream is not encrypted. Use a trusted network. Google Cast and the receiver may process device and connection information under their own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy. Casting does not upload your library to Double Perspective.

Ads (Zenith & Zero and Lirecho on Android)

Ads come from Google AdMob. Since November 15, 2026, they can be personalized:

  • Google may use your device's advertising ID to choose ads from your interests and ad history, and to measure them;
  • Android's ad-interest (Topics) and ad-measurement features stay turned off in our games.

To deliver and count each ad, Google also receives your IP address (which gives a general location), an app set ID that only links our own apps on your device, your device type and Android version, whether you saw or tapped the ad, and basic diagnostics. Google uses this to show the ad, limit repeats and prevent fraud (Google's AdMob data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy). We only see totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned.

Your choices: where the law requires it (for example in Europe), Google asks first, and a Privacy choices button lets you change your answer; if you decline, ads are not personalized. Anywhere, you can turn off ad personalization or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings (Google, then Ads). With Pro, or in apps without ads, no ad is ever requested. In Lirecho, you can also choose to watch one rewarded video to turn off ads for 24 hours. Test versions released up to November 7, 2026 (Zenith & Zero 2.1 and Cribbage 1.1.1, plus an early Umbral Purge Android test that is no longer updated) also used Google's standard ad settings; versions from November 8 to 14, 2026 requested non-personalized ads only.

Purchases and stores

Google Play and Steam handle downloads, payments and refunds under their own policies; we never see your card details. For Pro, the app only receives Google's confirmation that you own it, kept on your device so it works offline. Stores give us sales and crash totals, not personal profiles. See the Valve Privacy Policy for Steam.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives connection information such as IP addresses (GitHub Privacy Statement). There are no trackers, analytics or ad scripts. The language menu saves its setting only in your browser.

Children

Our games are for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly receive information from children; if you think a child emailed us, tell us and we'll delete it.
