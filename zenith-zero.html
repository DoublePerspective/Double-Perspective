Cards / 01

Solitaire

Your favourite card-table rituals, together in one collection.

  • Seven games: Klondike, FreeCell, Pyramid, Spider, TriPeaks, Hearts and Spades
  • Hearts and Spades against three calm computer players, with hints and a score sheet
  • Draw one or three in Klondike, with hints and undo
  • Classic illustrated cards, six card backs, scenic backgrounds and saved games
Numbers / 02

Sudoku

A proper number puzzle, with room to work things out.

  • Five difficulties, from Easy to Extreme
  • Notes, automatic notes, undo and helpful hints
  • Peer and matching-number highlights, with saved progress
Words / 03

Word Search

Follow a trail of letters to a satisfying discovery.

  • Three grid sizes: 8×8, 10×10 and 12×12
  • Six word themes and a daily puzzle
  • Drag across letters or tap the first and last letter; hints when needed
Words / 04

Crossword

Small clues. Clever connections. One word at a time.

  • Mini, Classic and Leisure puzzles with 6, 8 or 10 clues
  • Across/down clues and a built-in letter keyboard
  • Check a word, reveal a letter or undo your last move
  • A keyboard that follows your language and region
Tiles / 05

Mahjong

Find the open pairs in a beautifully layered board.

  • Eight layered layouts, from Meadow to Steps
  • 46 to 98 tiles, plus a Daily Grove arrangement
  • Hints, undo and shuffle, with larger-tile and highlight options
Picture logic / 06

Nonograms

A little logic. A picture revealed, one square at a time.

  • 63 original pictures in 5×5, 10×10 and 15×15, each solvable by logic alone
  • Guided tutorial, daily picture, logical hints, check, undo and redo
  • Tap or drag to fill and mark squares; pinch to zoom on big puzzles
  • A picture collection that fills in as you solve
Cards / 07

Cribbage

A classic board, a patient opponent, peg by peg.

  • Play one or two computer opponents, first to 121
  • The crib, pegging and an automatic count of every hand
  • A real pegboard in your colour: red, green or blue
  • Hints, game stats and saved games
